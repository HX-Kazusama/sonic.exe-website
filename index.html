ABOUT THE MOD

Ready to play once again...?

Friday Night Funkin': Vs Sonic.EXE (Unofficial) is a fan-made revival inspired by the iconic 2021 Vs. Sonic.EXE mod. Built to recapture the original's dark atmosphere and nostalgia, this project brings back part of the cancelled content with new reimaginations of its story, songs, and characters.

With eerie encounters, revamped visuals, and reimagined tracks, Vs Sonic.EXE (Unofficial) aims to deliver both a nostalgic and fresh experience for fans of the original mod.

  • 50+ remade songs
  • 20+ playable characters / opponents
  • Full achievement system with GameJolt trophies
  • Unique art & animations for every character
  • Multilanguage support

Our first post-launch update is planned to be Version 1.5, bringing the PARALLAX cast into the mod alongside the Christmas Special songs. We'd love to make this update a reality, and we're excited to continue expanding the experience with even more content.

Looking even further ahead, Version 2 is shaping up to be our biggest and most ambitious update yet. It will continue the Story Mode campaign with its second chapter, Overthrow, where you'll play as Girlfriend after she is mysteriously teleported during the Triple Trouble cutscene. Follow her journey as she faces new threats, uncovers hidden truths, and experiences the story from an entirely different perspective. This update will also introduce the remaining members of the Sound Test and File Extensions cast. Alongside new songs, mechanics, and surprises, our goal is to deliver an experience unlike anything we've created before.
